      Prototype Methodology

      Explainable engineering decision support

      This prototype combines DMA balance indicators, pressure, burst history, asset condition, acoustic suitability, operational response, data quality and intervention intensity.

      The scores are intentionally simplified and transparent. A production engine should be calibrated against confirmed leak/no-leak outcomes, recovered volume, sensor performance, hydraulic model outputs and utility-specific engineering rules.

      Important: the system recommends where and how to investigate. It does not replace field confirmation or authorize excavation.
